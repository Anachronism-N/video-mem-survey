\section{Conclusion}

This survey proposes a memory-centric view of video generation and video world models. Rather than treating long-form generation as a simple context-length problem, we organize the field around memory objects, memory substrates, and memory lifecycles. This perspective connects KV cache policies, RoPE correction, frequency-domain preservation, identity and entity memory, retrieval-augmented generation, and spatial/world-state memory. The next generation of long-video and world-model systems will likely depend not only on larger backbones, but on structured memory systems that can preserve, retrieve, update, and forget identities, scenes, events, and persistent world states.
